How the model works

  1. Route the runoff. Every 5 minutes, rainfall in mm is multiplied by effective catchment area in m² to get liters. The example model uses fixed runoff coefficients of 0.95 for roofs and 0.80 for pavement.
  2. Balance the water. Add runoff to the previous stored volume. Infiltrate up to the soil rate times the garden area for that interval, store up to area times ponding depth, then send the excess to the drain.
  3. Compare and size. The baseline sends all modeled runoff directly to the drain. Capture is storm-time infiltration plus water still stored at storm end. The size finder searches 0–500 m², keeping soil rate and depth fixed.

The model starts with an empty garden and omits rainfall directly on the bed, upstream losses beyond the runoff coefficients, and pipe or underdrain hydraulics. Rainfall patterns and sample sites are synthetic. Use measured inputs and professional site assessment for construction decisions.
